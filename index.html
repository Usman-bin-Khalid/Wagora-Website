Ask WAGORA

An assistant that knows your dog.

Ask anything, from "why is Max scratching?" to "help me prepare for the vet". Answers consider your dog's breed, age, allergies and recent days.

  • Food scanner. Photograph a label and check ingredients against your dog's allergies.
  • Document reading. Scan a vet paper and WAGORA explains it in plain words.
  • Weekly recap. A warm summary of your week together.
  • Your choice. Turn off personalised AI any time in Privacy settings.

General guidance, not veterinary advice. For anything worrying, always talk to your vet.

Why has Max been scratching more this week?

Here's what I found based on Max's recent behaviour and records.

What you've noticed

  • More frequent scratching
  • Mostly around ears and paws
  • Slight redness in photos

What to monitor

  • Redness, swelling or discharge
  • Changes in appetite or energy

When to contact a vet

  • If it worsens or lasts more than 3 days
  • If you see open sores or swelling

Questions

Good to know.

Something else on your mind? Visit the Support Centre or write to wagora.support@gmail.com. A real person reads every message.

Is WAGORA free?

Yes. WAGORA is free to download, and daily care, reminders, health records, safety tools and the community are free. WAGORA Plus adds advanced AI, deeper insights, unlimited dogs and more.

Do I need an account?

You can sign in with email, Google or Apple, or look around as a guest first. An account keeps your dog's records safe and synced across devices.

Is the AI a vet?

No. Ask WAGORA gives general guidance based on your dog's profile and recent days. It is never a diagnosis. For anything worrying, please contact your vet or an emergency clinic.

How does the lost-dog alert work?

Tap "Is your dog missing?", confirm the last-seen place and details, and WAGORA alerts people nearby. You also get a shareable poster. Locations are approximate, never exact.

What does the QR tag reveal about me?

Nothing private. Someone who scans it can contact you through a relay without seeing your phone number or home address.

Can my sitter or partner use it too?

Yes. Invite them and choose exactly what they can see, and until when. You can remove them at any time.

Is it on iPhone?

WAGORA is launching on Android first, with iPhone on the way.

How do I cancel Plus or delete my data?

Cancel in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. To delete your account, go to Profile → Privacy → Delete account, or read how deletion works.
