Getting started

How do I add my dog?

When you first open WAGORA, set-up asks for your dog's name, breed, birthday and a photo. Allergies and conditions are optional, but they make AI answers and the emergency card more useful. You can add more dogs later from the dog switcher on the Home screen. Free accounts include up to 2 dogs; WAGORA Plus is unlimited.

What can I log each day?

Food, water, walks, medication, weight and mood. Your care score on Home shows how much of today's care is done, and streaks reward the small habits. Everything you log feeds the wellness charts and the weekly insight.

How do reminders work?

Vaccination, medication and vet reminders follow the dates and schedules you set (daily, twice daily, weekly or monthly). Switch each type on or off in Profile → Settings → Notifications. Medication reminders are scheduled on your phone, so they work offline too.

Does WAGORA work offline?

Yes. You can keep logging and see your dog's records without a connection, and WAGORA syncs when you are back online. Things that need the internet, such as Ask WAGORA, the community and nearby places, will wait until you are connected.

Which phones and languages are supported?

WAGORA is on Android now, with iPhone on the way. The app is available in English, Español and Deutsch. Choose a language in Profile → Settings → Language, or follow your phone's language. Ask WAGORA answers in the language you choose.

Account & sign-in

How can I sign in?

With email and password, with Google or Apple, or as a guest to look around first. Create an account to keep your dog's records safe and synced across devices.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen tap Forgot password? and enter your email. If an account exists for it, a reset link is on its way. Check your spam folder if it does not arrive within a few minutes.

Can I use WAGORA on a new phone?

Sign in with the same account and your dogs, records and documents come with you. Guest data stays on the device it was created on, so create an account before switching phones.

Can my partner or sitter use it too?

Yes. Invite them from the sharing screen and choose exactly what they can see (feeding, medication, emergency details, documents) and until when. You can remove their access at any time. Free accounts can share with 1 family member or sitter; Plus has no limit.

WAGORA Plus & billing

What does WAGORA Plus include?

Unlimited dogs, advanced AI that knows your dog's full history, deeper trends and a weekly recap, smart document scanning with unlimited storage, vet reports for any period without a watermark, unlimited sharing with per-section permissions, expense charts and CSV export, and priority support. See the pricing section.

How much does it cost? Is there a trial?

Weekly $1.99, monthly $4.99 and yearly $34.99 (about $2.92 a month), in USD. The yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. Prices can vary slightly by country and are always shown in Google Play before you confirm.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Google Play, not by WAGORA. Open Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → WAGORA and tap Cancel subscription. Plus stays active until the end of the period you already paid for. Uninstalling the app does not cancel it.

I paid but Plus is not active

Open the WAGORA Plus screen and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same Google account that paid. If it still does not show, close and reopen the app, then email us with your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.) and we will sort it out.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds for Google Play purchases are handled by Google. You can request one from your Google Play order history. If something went wrong in the app, contact us and we will help.

Which features stay free?

Everything that keeps a dog safe stays free for everyone: lost-dog alerts, the emergency card, QR ID and hazard reports. The daily journal, reminders, health records and the community are free too.

Lost dog & safety

How do I raise a lost-dog alert?

Open Safety, choose Lost dog, confirm the last-seen place and the description, then tap Activate lost-dog alert. People nearby are notified within about a minute, and you can share a poster. Tap I found my dog as soon as they are home to end the alert.

What can someone see when they scan my dog's QR tag?

Your dog's name, photo and a way to contact you through a relay. They do not see your phone number or home address. You decide which emergency details (such as allergies) are visible.

How is my location used?

Only approximately. Public alerts and reports are rounded to about 1 km, and nearby alerts use a coarse area of about 5 km. WAGORA never stores your precise location or a location history. You can turn location access off in your phone settings; features that need it will simply ask.

How do I report a hazard?

Open the Safety map, tap Report, pick what you noticed (broken glass, toxic substance, unsafe area, aggressive dog, traffic danger), set the place and send. Hazard reports expire after 7 days so the map stays current.

My dog is hurt or ill. What now?

Call your vet or an emergency clinic straight away. WAGORA's Emergency mode shows allergies, medication, microchip number and your vet's number, and works offline. WAGORA gives general information and is never a replacement for a veterinarian.

Ask WAGORA (AI)

What can Ask WAGORA do?

Answer questions with your dog's breed, age, allergies and recent days in mind, scan food labels against allergies, read vet documents, help you prepare for a vet visit, suggest training plans and write a weekly recap.

Is the AI a replacement for my vet?

No. Answers are general guidance, not a diagnosis. WAGORA will tell you when something is worth a vet visit, but for anything worrying please contact your vet.

Why did I hit a daily limit?

To keep WAGORA free for everyone, AI chat, food scans and document scans have daily or weekly limits on the free plan. WAGORA Plus raises them a lot. Limits reset automatically.

Can I stop the AI using my dog's data?

Yes. Turn off personalised AI in Profile → Privacy settings. Answers will then use general knowledge only.

The food scanner got a label wrong

Make sure the ingredient list is in focus, well lit and fully in the frame, then try again. You can also choose a photo from your gallery. Always check the packaging yourself if your dog has serious allergies.

Privacy & data

How do I download my data?

Go to Profile → Privacy → Download my data. We email you a link that works for 7 days.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Privacy → Delete account. This is permanent: your profile, dogs, records, photos and files are deleted and the posts you made are removed. No app access? Follow the steps on the delete your account page.

Who can see my dog's records?

Only you and the caregivers you invite, and only the parts you choose. Community posts are visible to other WAGORA members, so only share what you want to share.

Do you sell my data or show ads?

Never. WAGORA has no ads, and we do not sell your data. Read the full Privacy Policy.

How do I block someone or report a post?

Open the menu on any post or profile to report it or block the person. Blocked people's posts and comments are hidden, and you can unblock them in Settings → Blocked people. Reported content is hidden automatically after several reports and reviewed by a person.

Troubleshooting

I'm not getting reminders

1) Check the type is on in Profile → Settings → Notifications. 2) Allow notifications for WAGORA in your phone settings. 3) On Android, tap Remind exactly on time and allow alarms so Android does not delay doses. 4) Turn off battery optimisation for WAGORA, as some phones pause background apps.

The app is slow or crashing

Update WAGORA from Google Play and restart your phone. If it still happens, clear the app cache (Android settings → Apps → WAGORA → Storage). Then email us with your phone model and Android version so we can fix it.

My photos or documents will not upload

Check your connection and that WAGORA has permission to use the camera and photos. Large files can take a moment on a slow network.

My data looks out of date

Pull down to refresh the screen. If you use more than one device, make sure both are online so they can sync.
