Privacy Policy

WAGORA helps you care for your dog: daily logs, health records, reminders, an AI assistant, safety tools and a community. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We wrote it to be read, not skimmed past.

The short version

  • We collect what the app needs to work for you and your dog, and nothing to sell. We never sell your data.
  • Your dog's records are private to you and the people you choose to share them with.
  • Location is only ever approximate (about 1 km for anything public) and only used when you use a feature that needs it.
  • You can download a copy of your data or delete your account at any time from Profile → Privacy.

What we collect

  • Account: your name, email, profile photo and phone number (optional), and how you sign in (email, Google, Apple or as a guest).
  • Your dogs: profile details (name, breed, birthday, weight, allergies, conditions, microchip number), photos, daily logs, health records (vaccinations, medications, vet visits, documents), expenses, reminders and training progress.
  • Community: posts, comments, reactions, group memberships and reports you make. Posts are visible to other WAGORA members.
  • Safety: lost and found alerts, hazard reports and sightings, with an approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) and the area name you choose.
  • Location: your approximate position when you open the safety map, nearby places or lost-and-found, and a coarse area (about 5 km) for nearby lost-dog alerts if you keep them on. We never store your precise location or a location history.
  • Device: a push-notification token, device platform and language, and crash reports (to fix problems).
  • Purchases: if you buy WAGORA Plus, the store (Google Play or the App Store) handles payment. We receive the subscription status, never your card details.

How we use it

  • To run the app: store your records, sync them across devices, send reminders and notifications you asked for.
  • AI features (Ask WAGORA, food scanner, document reading, weekly recap): the relevant part of your dog's records and the photo or question you send are processed to answer you. You can turn off personalised AI in Privacy settings; answers then use general knowledge only. AI answers are general information, not veterinary advice.
  • Safety and community: to show alerts and posts to people nearby and to keep the community kind (automated and human moderation of reported content).
  • Caregiver sharing: when you invite someone, they see only the parts you selected, until the end date you set or until you remove them.
  • To keep WAGORA secure and working: abuse prevention, fixing crashes, and counting usage in aggregate.

Who processes your data

We use trusted providers who process data on our behalf, under contract:

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud (USA): accounts, database, file storage, notifications, crash reports, backups.
  • Google Gemini API: AI answers and analysis (on paid terms, which do not use your content to train Google's models).
  • Google Maps Platform (Places): nearby vets and dog-friendly places (we send an approximate area, not your identity).
  • Render (USA): hosts the WAGORA server.
  • Google Play / Apple App Store: subscriptions and payments.

We share information with other people only as you direct (posts, alerts, caregivers), or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and dog records: until you delete them or your account.
  • Notifications: 90 days. Hazard reports: 7 days. Lost and found alerts: 30 days after their last update.
  • Data exports: the download link works for 7 days.
  • Backups: up to 30 days, then permanently deleted.
  • When you delete your account, your profile, dogs, records, photos and files are deleted; posts you made are removed.

Your choices and rights

  • See and download your data: Profile → Privacy → Download my data. We email you a link.
  • Delete your account: Profile → Privacy → Delete account. This is permanent.
  • Control AI and analytics: Profile → Privacy settings.
  • Control notifications and alerts: Profile → Settings → Notifications.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you also have rights to correct, restrict or object to processing, and to complain to your data-protection authority.

Children

WAGORA is not meant for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect their data.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is limited by strict rules: your dog's records can be read only by you and the caregivers you invite.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will tell you in the app. Questions or requests: privacy@wagora.app.
